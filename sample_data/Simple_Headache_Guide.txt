Simple Health Guide: Headaches and Migraines

Types of Common Headaches:
1. Tension Headache: The most common type. Causes a dull, aching pain or a feeling of a tight band wrapped around the forehead. Usually caused by stress, lack of sleep, or eye strain.
2. Migraine: A severe, throbbing headache, usually on one side of the head. Often accompanied by nausea, vomiting, and extreme sensitivity to light and sound. Some people experience visual 'auras' (flashing lights or blind spots) before a migraine begins.

Common Migraine Triggers:
- Stress and anxiety
- Skipping meals or dehydration
- Lack of sleep or irregular sleep schedule
- Specific foods (aged cheeses, chocolate, processed meats with nitrates, caffeine withdrawal)
- Bright flashing lights or loud noises

Basic Relief & Home Remedies:
- Rest in a quiet, dark room with a cool cloth on the forehead.
- Drink plenty of water to stay hydrated.
- Over-the-counter pain relievers: Acetaminophen, Ibuprofen, or Naproxen (use as directed on the label).

Red Flag Warning Signs (Seek Urgent Care):
Seek immediate medical attention if a headache is sudden and explosive ('thunderclap' headache), or occurs with a high fever, stiff neck, confusion, numbness, or difficulty speaking.
