Simple Health Guide: Vitamin D & Bone Health

Why is Vitamin D Important?
Vitamin D helps the body absorb calcium, which is essential for building and maintaining strong bones, healthy teeth, and supporting the immune system.

Daily Recommended Intake:
- Children and adults (ages 1 to 70): 600 IU (15 micrograms) daily.
- Adults over age 70: 800 IU (20 micrograms) daily.
- Safe upper daily limit for adults: 4,000 IU per day.

Primary Sources:
1. Sunlight: The body makes Vitamin D when direct sunlight hits the skin (15-20 minutes a day is often sufficient).
2. Food Sources: Fatty fish (salmon, tuna, mackerel), egg yolks, fortified milk, fortified orange juice, and breakfast cereals.
3. Supplements: Vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol) supplements are recommended for people with limited sun exposure.

Deficiency Symptoms:
- Frequent infections or illnesses
- Fatigue and tiredness
- Bone and lower back pain
- Muscle weakness or cramps
- In severe cases, soft bones (osteomalacia in adults, rickets in children)
